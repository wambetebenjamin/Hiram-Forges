# Image credits

Every photograph listed here is a real workshop photograph published on Pexels under
the Pexels licence. No image on this site was generated by artificial intelligence and
no watermarked preview is used. Files live in `public/images/`.

## How the hero images were made

The hero keeps the uploaded theme exactly. The yellow field, the hexagon pattern, the
swoosh curve and the type positions are the theme asset. Only the photographic area
inside the swoosh was replaced with three Pexels forge photographs, toned to sit under
the theme dark type. The curve itself is untouched.

Where a photographer is not named below, the credit is on the linked Pexels page.
